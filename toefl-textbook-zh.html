开始之前 · 先认准新版

2026 新托福考什么？

新版不适合继续套用旧版“阅读长文章＋四道口语＋综合写作”的训练顺序。四科任务如下：

阅读｜约 30 分钟，50 题

Complete the Words（补全单词）· Read in Daily Life（日常阅读）· Read an Academic Passage（学术阅读）

听力｜约 29 分钟，47 题

Listen and Choose a Response（听句选回应）· Conversation（对话）· Announcement（通知）· Academic Talk（学术讲解）

写作｜约 23 分钟，12 题

Build a Sentence（组句）· Write an Email（写邮件）· Write for an Academic Discussion（学术讨论）

口语｜约 8 分钟，11 题

Listen and Repeat（听后复述）· Take an Interview（模拟采访）

分数也变了

成绩采用 1–6 分制，以 0.5 分为一个档位。过渡期内，成绩单还会同时提供可比的 0–120 总分。

户外活动、旅行习惯、阅读习惯、饮食习惯、运动习惯、兴趣与休闲。每套 4 问，通常按“个人事实 → 偏好与理由 → 经历或比较 → 较广泛的观点”推进，每问约 45 秒作答。

资料同源改编｜采访练习

说说你最常做的一项休闲活动。你更喜欢独自进行还是和朋友一起？请举一个真实经历说明原因。最后回答：兴趣爱好是不是幸福生活中必不可少的一部分？

第一章 · Reading

阅读：短、快、题型多。

新阅读既有生活场景，也有学术短文。先判断文本用途，再找信息；补词题要同时看词义、拼写和语法。

题型一｜Complete the Words

练习 1

Many city parks prov___ residents with a quiet place to relax. Trees also red___ summer heat by creating shade.

请补全两个单词

prov___ = ______　　red___ = ______

答案

provide；reduce。主语 parks 和 Trees 都是复数。不要只凭字母猜，还要检查句子是否通顺。

题型二｜Read in Daily Life

图书馆通知

Weekend Study Room Update
The second-floor study rooms will be closed this Saturday morning for equipment checks. Rooms on the third floor will remain open, but reservations are recommended. Normal service will resume at 1:00 p.m.

练习 2

Why are the second-floor rooms unavailable?

  1. They have already been reserved.
  2. The library is checking equipment.
  3. The third floor is being repaired.
  4. The library closes at 1:00 p.m.
练习 3

What should a student do if they need a room on Saturday morning?

答案与解释

2：B。通知直接说明 for equipment checks。
3：Use a third-floor room and reserve it in advance. 三楼开放，但建议预约。

题型三｜Read an Academic Passage

学术短文

Bees are often associated with honey production, but their larger ecological role is pollination. As bees move between flowers, they carry pollen that allows many plants to reproduce. Some crops can also be pollinated by wind or other animals, yet bees remain especially efficient because they repeatedly visit the same kind of flower during a single trip. This behavior increases the chance that pollen reaches a compatible plant.

练习 4｜主旨

What is the passage mainly about?

  1. Why honey is difficult to produce
  2. How bees support plant reproduction
  3. Why wind harms agricultural crops
  4. How flowers attract different animals
练习 5｜原因

Why does the author mention that bees visit the same kind of flower repeatedly?

  1. To explain why bees are effective pollinators
  2. To show that bees avoid most plants
  3. To compare honey from different flowers
  4. To argue that wind pollination is unnecessary
答案与解释

4：B。全文中心是蜜蜂授粉及其作用。
5：A。最后一句说明这种行为让花粉更可能到达相容植物。

第二章 · Listening

听力：先听目的，再抓关系。

正式考试录音只播放一次。下面用文字稿练题型；练习时最好请同学读一遍，或者用朗读工具播放后再答题。

题型一｜Listen and Choose a Response

你听到

“I thought the workshop started at ten.”

练习 6
  1. It was moved to ten thirty.
  2. I enjoy workshops, too.
  3. The room has ten chairs.
  4. Yes, I started the project.
答案

A。说话人以为十点开始，最自然的回应是解释时间已经改到十点半。

题型二｜Listen to a Conversation

对话稿

Student: I uploaded my assignment last night, but the course page says it’s missing.
Instructor: I can see the file, but it’s in the draft folder. Open the assignment page and click “Submit.” You don’t need to upload it again.

练习 7

What is the student’s problem?

  1. The file was deleted.
  2. The assignment was saved but not submitted.
  3. The instructor cannot open the file.
  4. The student uploaded the wrong assignment.
练习 8

What does the instructor tell the student to do?

答案

7：B。文件在 draft folder。
8：Open the assignment page and click “Submit.” 不用重新上传。

题型三｜Listen to an Announcement

通知稿

Attention passengers: The 4:15 train to Harbor City will depart from Platform 6 instead of Platform 4. The departure time has not changed. Please use the stairs near the information desk to reach the new platform.

练习 9

What changed?

  1. The destination
  2. The departure time
  3. The platform
  4. The ticket price
练习 10

Where should passengers go to reach the train?

答案

9：C。从 4 号站台改到 6 号站台。
10：Use the stairs near the information desk.

题型四｜Listen to an Academic Talk

学术讲解稿

Some desert plants open tiny pores on their leaves only at night. Because nighttime temperatures are lower, less water escapes while the pores are open. The plant stores the carbon dioxide it takes in and uses it for photosynthesis during the day, when the pores remain closed. This unusual timing helps the plant conserve water.

练习 11

Why do the plants open their pores at night?

  1. To absorb more sunlight
  2. To reduce water loss
  3. To release stored carbon dioxide
  4. To increase leaf temperature
练习 12

What happens during the day?

答案

11：B。夜间较凉，水分流失少。
12：The pores stay closed, and the plant uses stored carbon dioxide for photosynthesis.

第三章 · Writing

写作：从组句到真实沟通。

新版写作没有旧版“阅读＋讲座”的综合写作。你需要完成组句、邮件和学术讨论。

题型一｜Build a Sentence

练习 13

the museum / because of heavy rain / closed early / yesterday

练习 14

students to submit / the professor / their outlines / asked / by Friday

答案

13：The museum closed early yesterday because of heavy rain.
14：The professor asked students to submit their outlines by Friday.

题型二｜Write an Email

练习 15｜邮件任务

You joined a campus photography club, but the meeting time conflicts with one of your classes. Write an email to the club leader. Explain the problem, ask whether another group meets at a different time, and suggest one way you could still help the club.

自然范文

Subject: Question About the Photography Club Schedule

Hi Maya,

I’m excited to join the photography club, but I just noticed that the Tuesday meeting overlaps with my chemistry class. Is there another group that meets later in the week?

If Tuesday is the only option, I could still help by editing photos for the club’s social media page or by preparing captions after each event. I’d really like to stay involved even if I can’t attend every meeting.

Thanks for your help,
Leo

为什么这封邮件有效

第一段直接说明冲突并提出问题；第二段给出可行方案。语气礼貌，但没有堆砌生硬套话。

题型三｜Write for an Academic Discussion

练习 16｜课堂讨论

Professor: Some universities record every lecture and make the videos available to students. Should all lectures be recorded?

Nina: Yes. Recordings help students review difficult ideas and support anyone who misses class because of illness.

Omar: No. If every lecture is online, some students may stop attending and participate less.

自然范文

Nina makes a good point about reviewing difficult material. I think lectures should be recorded, but teachers should still give students a reason to attend in person. For example, the recording could include the main lecture while small-group discussions and short in-class activities are available only to students who are present. This approach gives sick students access to essential content without turning the course into a passive video library. It also lets students replay complicated explanations before an exam.

再练一题

Professor: Should first-year university students be required to live on campus?

写一段回应：明确立场，回应一个同学可能提出的观点，再补充一个具体理由或例子。

第四章 · Speaking

口语：复述准确，采访自然。

新版口语只有两类任务。重点不是背万能模板，而是让对方听清楚：发音、重音、节奏、语法和意思都要稳定。

题型一｜Listen and Repeat

练法

一句话分成意群听：After the lecture / you can pick up the worksheet / at the front desk. 先记意思块，再模仿重音和停顿，不要一个词一个词机械拼接。

练习 17｜由短到长，每句只听一次
  1. The library closes at nine.
  2. Please leave your completed form at the front desk.
  3. Students who missed yesterday’s lecture can watch the recording online.
  4. Although the main entrance is being repaired, visitors can enter through the door beside the café.
自查

录下自己的声音。每句检查三件事：有没有漏掉关键信息；动词和复数结尾是否清楚；停顿是否按意思分组。

题型二｜Take an Interview

采访主题：学习空间

Imagine you are being interviewed about places where students study.

练习 18｜连续回答
  1. Where do you usually study, and why?
  2. What makes a study space comfortable?
  3. Tell me about a time when your study environment affected your work.
  4. Do you think universities will need more quiet spaces in the future? Why or why not?
示范回答 1

I usually study in a small library near my apartment because it is quiet and has large desks. At home, I’m easily distracted by cooking or messages on my phone, but the library helps me stay focused for an hour or two.

示范回答 3

Last semester, I tried to prepare for an exam in a crowded café. The music was loud, and people kept walking past my table, so I read the same page several times without understanding it. I moved to an empty classroom and finished much faster. That experience showed me that a calm environment makes a real difference.

示范回答 4

Yes, I think universities will need more quiet spaces. Students now use campuses for online classes, group projects, and independent study, so one large library is often not enough. Small rooms in different buildings would let students study between classes without crossing the entire campus.

“Could you send me the notes from yesterday’s class?”

  1. Sure, I’ll email them tonight.
  2. The class is in Room 12.
  3. I wrote about climate change.
  4. Yesterday was a busy day.
4｜Build a Sentence

the results / until next week / will not announce / the research team

5｜Write an Email
