Part 2 · Everyday phrases

Use these when English feels too fast.

These polite phrases help you keep a conversation going instead of giving up.

Could you say that again?Polite and natural when you need repetition.
你可以再说一遍吗？
Could you speak more slowly?Use this when the speed is difficult.
你可以说慢一点吗？
What does this mean?Point to a word or sentence while asking.
这个是什么意思？
I don’t understand yet.“Yet” suggests that you are still learning.
我还不明白。
