Meet the founder

Hi, I’m Lizzy.

Living in different places made me curious about language and the culture behind it. Language is a big part of my life, and helping others connect with new people and places makes this work meaningful.

I named this website GUINEA PIG LANGUAGE because I have five guinea pigs. I work with native speakers to make every language trustworthy, and as I experience more languages and cultures, I hope to add more.

おすすめは何ですか？

Osusume wa nan desu ka?

What do you recommend?

A natural, polite question you can use in a restaurant or shop in Japan.

¿Qué me recomienda?

keh meh reh-koh-MYEN-dah

What do you recommend?

A polite and natural way to ask for a recommendation in Spain.

您推荐什么？

Nín tuījiàn shénme?

What do you recommend?

Using 您 keeps the question polite when speaking to staff or someone you do not know.
