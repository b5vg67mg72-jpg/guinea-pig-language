Meet the creator

Made in Japan with native-speaker collaboration.

Hi, I’m Lizzy, the founder of GUINEA PIG LANGUAGE. Living in Japan gives me daily contact with Japanese language and culture, as well as a trusted circle of native Japanese friends who help bring these materials to life.

I started Morumotto to make the first stage of language learning feel clearer, friendlier, and less overwhelming. I shape each product around the needs of international beginners, while native Japanese collaborators help develop the language, natural expressions, practical examples, and cultural context.

How the materials are developed

Lizzy plans the learning journey and beginner-friendly presentation. Native Japanese collaborators contribute and check the Japanese used in the lessons so examples feel natural and useful in everyday situations.

How Morumotto is different from a traditional textbook

01

One clear goal at a time

Focused resources help you make visible progress without trying to absorb an entire language at once.

02

Everyday Japanese first

Lessons prioritize useful situations, natural wording, and examples you can imagine using in real life.

03

Practice, not information overload

Guided activities help you write, recall, and build language instead of only reading explanations.

04

Designed for independent beginners

Friendly layouts, romaji support where useful, and small steps make it easier to study on your own.

A useful companion—not a replacement for every textbook. Traditional textbooks can provide comprehensive long-term study. Morumotto gives beginners an approachable starting point and practical, focused practice alongside deeper learning.
