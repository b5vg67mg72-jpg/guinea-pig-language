Local from the first draft

Mandarin built around sound, meaning, and life in China.

Native Mandarin speakers in China create and check the language throughout this collection. Pinyin, tone marks, characters, grammar, and context are reviewed together so the learning layers agree.

  • Every pinyin syllable is checked against its character and tone.
  • Examples use natural Mandarin word order rather than English logic.
  • Politeness and social context are explained without stereotypes.
  • Vocabulary reflects contemporary daily use in mainland China.

请问，地铁站怎么走？

Qǐngwèn, dìtiě zhàn zěnme zǒu?

Excuse me, how do I get to the metro station?

请问 politely introduces a question. 怎么走 asks how to get somewhere, not simply where it is.
