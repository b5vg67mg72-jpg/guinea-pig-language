Local from the first draft

Spanish shaped by real voices in Spain.

Native Spanish speakers in Spain create and check the language throughout this collection. Where usage differs across the Spanish-speaking world, we label that difference instead of pretending there is only one Spanish.

  • Dialogues are written for natural interaction, not literal translation.
  • Tone, register, and everyday word choice are reviewed locally.
  • Pronunciation guidance reflects the Spanish standard being taught.
  • Regional alternatives are noted when they matter to beginners.

¿Nos pone dos cafés, por favor?

nohs POH-neh dohs kah-FEHS, por fah-VOR

Could we have two coffees, please?

In Spain, poner is a natural verb when ordering in a café. A literal English translation would miss that everyday usage.
